mock-data qualification only: passed on independently authored synthetic fixtures and synthetic workloads, Windows x64, CPython 3.12

Author: Architect, 2026-10-30. Status: SKELETON for the PO ruling pass; shapes only, value choices are RECOMMENDED rulings in M3A_RULING_LIST.md. Written once so that the structural codes and the precedence lists exist from the start (lesson of M2: precedence, type-first per field and trusted-versus-untrusted inputs were reworked four times after the fixtures). Baseline: docs/m2/M2_IO_CONTRACT.md and the M1 code interface (`chainiv.py`, `quotes.py`). Pure functions, no I/O, no clocks, single-threaded kernels.

## 1. Conventions (one convention per record type)
- Chain record type: the VALUES are the M1 values (`to_ticks`: a text decimal with at most 8 decimal places, or a non-bool int meaning whole units, x 10^8 ticks internally) but the validation is STRICTER than M1, per the one-convention-per-record-type rule. Explicit validation step for strike, bid, ask (text): the M2 price grammar `-?(\\d+(\\.\\d*)?|\\.\\d+)` on ASCII text ("1." and ".5" valid); M1 `to_ticks` would also accept " 1", "+1" and "1_0" through `int()`; these are rejected here, as is bool True (a bool is not an int); more than 8 decimals, a failed grammar or any other type is `malformed_type`. The M2 ticks rule (price x 10^4) applies ONLY to print families and is not used here.
- Request scalars (RULED input forms, M1 `to_ticks` and `float()` behaviour made explicit). `spot`: decimal text (grammar above, at most 8 places), a non-bool int, or a float or Fraction exactly representable in 8 decimals (M1 `to_ticks` accepts only those); "a/b" text and non-representable floats such as 100.1 are rejected as `malformed_type` (representation and grammar, consistent with chain strikes and quotes): text with more than 8 places, text failing the grammar, a float or Fraction not exactly 8-decimal-representable, or a wrong type; `bad_spot` is ONLY a value code (spot not finite or not > 0). `rate` and `dividend_yield`: a non-bool int, decimal text, a float or a Fraction (no "a/b" text, which is `malformed_type`); not finite or out of range is `bad_rate`. Ranges (RULED): `rate` finite with |rate| <= 1; `dividend_yield` finite with 0 <= dividend_yield <= 1; `spot` finite and > 0. A rate or dividend_yield outside its range or non-finite is `bad_rate`; a non-positive or non-finite spot is `bad_spot`.
- History records and parameters: the M2 conventions (type-first per field; integers for sessions, minutes, K).
- Times: integer nanoseconds (UTC); `expiry_days` is an integer >= 1 supplied by the caller (M1: expiry N x 24 h after the cutoff, T = N/365). No civil-date conversion in M3a (an adapter duty, RQ item).
- IV: annual decimal as returned by M1 (binary64); thresholds and differences in points (1 point = 0.01 annual vol).
- Text: UTF-8, LF, canonical key order, no absolute paths; every dataset has `"provenance": "SYNTHETIC"`; non-JSON types in fixtures use the M2 tagged objects (R-65).

## 2. Inputs
| Record | Fields | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Chain contract | EXACTLY the M1 `ChainContract`: `contract_id`, `expiry_days`, `right` ("call" or "put"), `strike`, `bid`, `ask`, `event_ns`, `known_at_ns` (None = event_ns), `style` ("european", "american"), `condition` ("regular" default) | no multiplier, no civil-date expiry, no lane on the chain record |
| Chain | `contracts`, `spot`, `rate`, `dividend_yield`, `cutoff_ns` | one chain at one cutoff |
| Request | `mode` in {"intraday", "close"}, `lane` in {"stock", "etf_index"}, `stale_limit_ns` (default 10 s), `feed_delay_tag` ("live" or "delayed"), `evaluation_ns`, `tenors`, `delta_target`, `iv_form` ("atm" default, or "30d"; V3 and V4), `delta_convention` ("spot" default, or "forward"; V8 to V11, V21 and the 25-delta points of M3A-0), `direction`, thresholds, X, `min_sessions` (20), `max_sessions` (252), `minute`, `prior_sessions` (1), `min_strikes`, `min_coverage` | every parameter used is echoed in the result |
| Prior reading | `value`, `kind` ("frame" or "session"), `time_ns` or `session_id` | the reading time must be before the cutoff (`prior_not_before_cutoff`) |
| History record | M2 `HistoryRecord`, lane only here; scalar series: `window_s` 0, empty grid, `total` = integer in 10^-8 units; minute series: dense grid | read as an M2 view, `scalar_baseline` and `same_minute_column` (matrix section 6) |
| Calendar | ordered sessions with `minutes` (M2) | the prior sessions come from it |
Session-close frame = the frame evaluated at `close_ns` on the close chain, stored as a scalar (not as a grid minute).

## 3. Outputs
The M2 `Result` envelope: `output_id` (M3A-0 to M3A-11), `series_id`, `session_id`, `evaluation_ns`, `status`, `reason` (closed set, matrix section 5, or null), `code` (structural, or null), `value`, `fired`, `params`, `coverage`, `labels` (list), `lane`, `version`. An unavailable or invalid_input result carries `value` null and `fired` null.
Number forms (RULED): quote-derived exact values (counts, coverage ratios) are ints or reduced "n/d" strings; solver-derived values (IV, forward, derived series) are binary64 and are reported as decimal strings rounded half to even to 8 decimals; derived series (skew25, term_30_91) are the difference of the UNROUNDED floats rounded once to 8 decimals; all threshold and sign decisions use the rounded decimals; stored history values are integers in 10^-8 units. Canonical hashes of solver-derived values are Windows-only (no cross-platform byte claim).
`coverage`: `listed_strikes`, `eligible_strikes`, per-expiry counts, and `contract_reasons` = {M1 gate reason: count} (per-contract counts have their own field); `labels`: the M1 label of the model used ("exact-model" or "approximate: European model on an American-style contract"), plus "delayed".

| M3A-0 | `items` (shape RATIFIED, section 8): `expiries` map keyed by `expiry_days` of {`status`, `reason`, `forward`, `atm_iv`, `put25_iv`, `call25_iv`}; `tenors` map {`9`, `30`, `91`: {`status`, `reason`, `iv`, `method`, `expiries_used`}} |
| M3A-1 V1 | `x`, `n`, `rank_le`, `upper_share`, `lower_share`, `form` |
| M3A-2 V3 | `iv_form`, `x`, `n`, `minute`, `rank_le`, `upper_share` |
| M3A-3 V4 | `iv_form`, `now`, `prior`, `change_points`, `change_ratio`, `prior_kind` |
| M3A-4 V5 | `pairs` {`9_30`, `30_91`: {`longer`, `shorter`, `spread_points`}} (longer minus shorter) |
| M3A-5 V6 | `nearest_days`, `second_days`, `spread_points` (second minus nearest) |
| M3A-6 V7 | `now`, `priors` (list of {`session_id`, `gap`}), `gap_change_points` for the n-th prior; no slope key |
| M3A-7 V8 | `put25_iv`, `call25_iv`, `skew_points` (put minus call), `tenor` |

Fire rules: comparisons inclusive ("at least X", "at most X") on the rounded decimals and the `direction` parameter (up, down, either); percentile rule as M2 for top X percent (upper_share <= X/100) and bottom X percent (lower_share <= X/100).

## 4. Structural codes and precedence lists (one list PER ENTRY POINT, as M2)
Structural errors are status invalid_input with a closed `code`; the first failing code of the entry point list is reported. Type-first per field (M2 ruling): a wrong-typed field reports `malformed_type` at the position where that field value check would run; a field with no value code reports it at the `malformed_type` slot; within one position fields are checked in the order written. M1 raises ValueError for these conditions; the M3a wrapper maps them to codes.
**Request (chain and parameters), 18 positions (position 10 holds two codes, position 13 three and position 17 two, in the order written, so 22 codes and 21 adjacent code pairs):**
| # | Code | Check |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `bad_cutoff` | cutoff_ns not a non-negative int |
| 2 | `bad_spot` | VALUE code: spot not finite or not > 0; a representation or type error of spot (more than 8 places, non-representable float or Fraction, wrong type) is `malformed_type` at this position |
| 3 | `bad_rate` | rate not finite or |rate| > 1; dividend_yield not finite or outside 0 to 1 |
| 4 | `invalid_mode` | mode not in {intraday, close} |
| 5 | `invalid_lane` | lane not in {stock, etf_index} |
| 6 | `bad_expiry` | expiry_days not an int >= 1 (as M1; per contract) |
| 7 | `unknown_right` | right not call or put |
| 8 | `duplicate_contract` | a contract id appears twice |
| 9 | `bad_strike` | strike not positive |
| 10 | `malformed_type`, then `cutoff_mismatch` | malformed_type: quote fields, condition, style, event times and every field without a value code; more than 8 decimals. cutoff_mismatch (a VALUE code, M2 reserves malformed_type for wrong types): `evaluation_ns` differs from the chain `cutoff_ns`; checked after the type checks of the same slot |

| 14 | `threshold_required` | a fire rule asked without a threshold |

**Snapshot:** snapshot_bad_magic, snapshot_length_mismatch, snapshot_hash_mismatch; **text load:** trailer_missing, trailer_count_mismatch, trailer_hash_mismatch (M2, unchanged).
**View read (`scalar_baseline`, `same_minute_column`):** session_not_in_calendar (current session), minute_out_of_range, malformed_type (min_sessions, max_sessions), threshold_out_of_range, convention_mismatch (a skew-type series of the other convention, R-160) (5 positions).
An empty chain, a chain whose contracts are all excluded, or a missing history is not structural: unavailable with the reasons of the matrix.

## 5. Trusted versus untrusted inputs
- Untrusted (validated in full at the boundary): chains from a caller, history records at append and load, parameters. Every code of section 4 applies.
- Trusted (validated once, then scanned on hot paths): views of a store whose content hash was verified at attach, and chains already validated by `validate="full"`. `validate="scanned"` is the hot-path default; `validate="full"` is always available with the same codes (M2 convention). Cost: scanned is O(contracts read), full is O(chain).

## 6. Canonical serialization and hashing
As M2 section 4: one JSON object per result in the key order of section 3, separators `,` and `:`, one object per line, LF; ordered by (output_id, series_id, session_id, evaluation_ns); per-unit SHA-256 and the run hash over `unit key LF unit sha256 LF` in key order. Worker count, timings, cache statistics and store hashes appear only in the run log. Result `version` is the library version string (no bump proposed, R-64).

## 7. Runner interface (unchanged defaults)
Unit = underlying x session x cutoff; the unit reads an immutable history view; opt-in process pool, default single-process, `--workers 1` is the reference path; cap per PR 72; the runner adds no calculation; `gc.freeze()` after the store load and `content_hash()` compared after attach.

## 8. Second ruling batch: shapes and rules (RULED 2026-10-30 (PO second batch), interim pending design ratification where the design is silent)
Rulings R-103 to R-127 of M3A_RULING_LIST.md, stated once here for the Tester and the Developer.
1. M3A-0 value shape (RATIFIED interim, R-108, amended by R-141 and R-142). The top-level `value` of M3A-0 is null; the frame details live in the `frame` object (declared here): `frame.expiries` map keyed by `expiry_days` of {`status`, `reason`, `forward`, `atm_iv`, `put25_iv`, `call25_iv`} where `put25_iv` and `call25_iv` are each {`status`, `reason`, `value`}; `frame.tenors[tenor]` = {`status`, `reason`, `iv`, `method`, `expiries_used`} with `method` in {`as_is`, `variance_interpolation`} (the M1 names). Frame status (R-141): the frame is available only when EVERY listed expiry, EVERY requested tenor and EVERY 25-delta side is available; otherwise the frame is unavailable and the per-expiry, per-tenor and per-side values are still shown. Frame reason = the first failing item in the order: expiries ascending (the lowest failing expiry reason), then tenors ascending, then 25-delta sides (put, then call). A 25-delta failure on one side therefore makes the frame unavailable although the expiry items stay shown (this follows the rows, COV-17 against COV-31 of author1). `coverage` = {`listed_strikes`, `eligible_strikes`, `per_expiry` map of {`listed_strikes`, `eligible_strikes`}, `contract_reasons`}; the totals are the sums of the per-expiry counts.
2. Coverage terms (R-103, supersedes the R-83 wording): listed strike = a strike with ANY contract record in the chain; eligible strike = a strike with at least one eligible quote (either right); `min_strikes` counts strikes, not contracts; the near-the-money clause (at least 2 eligible strikes per right within +-10 percent of the forward, inclusive) is a coverage gate. Inside one expiry `no_atm_strike` is checked before `low_surface_coverage`; a coverage-excluded expiry is treated like an expiry without an ATM strike in tenor interpolation (R-104).
3. A solver failure on one contract excludes that contract (its reason from the closed solver set is counted in `coverage.contract_reasons`); the expiry continues if coverage still holds (R-109).
4. Labels (R-105): a result on a mixed European/American frame carries both model labels as a sorted list; an unavailable result carries `labels` = []. An empty chain or a chain where no contract passes the gates is unavailable with `no_eligible_contract` (R-106).
5. Types and codes: a text or float `expiry_days` is `malformed_type`, a wrong value is `bad_expiry`; a wrong-typed cutoff_ns, expiry_days, mode, lane or right is `malformed_type` (R-107). The structural code field is `code`; non-structural unavailability uses `reason` (R-123). None bid or ask is a missing quote (`invalid_quote`, not structural) (R-124). Non-finite floats in fixtures use {"py_type": "float", "text": "nan" or "inf" or "-inf"} (R-125).
6. Request fields (R-115, R-119, R-120): `prior_kind` in {frame, session} for V10 with the condition per `direction`; `pair` in {9-30, 30-91} for V5; `delta_target` in {0.25} only (`delta_not_allowed` otherwise); threshold parameter names `at_least`, `at_most` and `x` (percentile). Positions 16 to 18 of the request list apply only to outputs that use that parameter. `threshold_out_of_range` also applies at view read for `x` (R-122). Three pair rows that cannot hold both errors are equivalent swaps (R-121).
7. History reads (R-110 to R-114, R-117, R-118): `max_sessions` = the last N available sessions; a history lane differing from the request lane at read is `insufficient_history` (`lane_mismatch` applies at append only); `same_minute_column` skips a session whose grid is shorter than the minute index (no zero-padding); the store declares per-series k with `store.add_series(series_id, k)` (k up to 252); fewer than n priors is `insufficient_history`; a bracketing expiry lacking a 25-delta bracket gives `no_delta_bracket` for the tenor. Structural request codes (including `threshold_required` at position 14 and `prior_not_before_cutoff` at position 17) are reported BEFORE any evaluation (R-131). Among result-level reasons only, the order for simultaneous conditions is: upstream and frame reasons, `insufficient_history`, `session_not_complete`, `zero_range_history`; then prior-reading checks; then the threshold.
8. Derived units (R-116): `change_ratio` is unitless (change/prior); V21 distance keys are in points (x100); V21 has no default threshold (`threshold_required` when none).
9. Solver boundary prices (R-128, corrected by R-145 to match inverse.py): `price_out_of_bounds` = target < ll or target > uh, where ll is the low end of the lower arb enclosure and uh the high end of the upper enclosure (invalid_input). The exact-bound statements hold ONLY when the bound is exact: a target exactly at an exact lower bound is available with IV 0.0, a target exactly at an exact upper bound is `upper_asymptote` (nonidentifiable_inverse). The lower bound is exact when r = q = 0, or S = K and r = q, or for an OTM forward where the lower bound is 0; the upper bound is exact for a call with q = 0 and for a put with r = 0. With non-zero rates a target inside the enclosure ends is `bound_precision` (no IV 0.0). `sigma_cap`, `precision_exhausted`, `bound_precision` and `iteration_budget` are not fixture-covered (solver internals needed); they are covered at unit level in the Developer tests with a stubbed solver; the gap is recorded.
10. Series metrics and read results (R-129, R-146, amended by R-153): metrics `atm_iv` (the nearest-eligible-expiry ATM IV MINUTE series on the session grid), `atm_iv_close` (session-close scalar), `iv30` (the 30-day IV minute series), `iv30_close` (scalar), `skew25` (scalar), `term_30_91` (scalar); the earlier "atm_iv has no consumer" note is resolved (V3 and V4 atm form); `series_id` = "<UNDERLYING>/<lane>/<metric>"; V4 prior readings and V3 history are read from the metric of the selected form, and a prior or history record of the other form is a different series (no mixing); `scalar_baseline` and `same_minute_column` return {`values`, `sessions`, `n`}.
11. Equivalent pair swaps (R-121, R-130): four precedence pair rows cannot hold both errors in one input; the fourth is (`minute_out_of_range`, `prior_not_before_cutoff`).
12. Second-batch rules from the oracle review (R-131 to R-140): the `labels` list is the lexicographic ASCII sort including "delayed" (R-132); `down` fires at x <= -X for change forms and V21 `down` fires at ratio <= X (R-133); `prior_not_before_cutoff` checks ALL supplied priors (R-134); V10 `condition` in {change, sign_change}, default `change` (R-135); an empty prior store is `no_prior_reading`, fewer than n priors (non-empty) is `insufficient_history`, for V7 and V10 alike (R-136); V11 and V21 failure precedence: ATM tenor, then put side, then call side (R-137); a coverage-excluded bracketing expiry gives `no_bracketing_expiry` for the tenor, a bracketing expiry lacking a 25-delta bracket gives `no_delta_bracket` (R-139); request vocabulary: threshold names `at_least`, `at_most`, `x` only, V5 `pair` values "9-30" and "30-91", `gating` = false on near-threshold rows (R-140). Tolerances (R-138): 3e-5 annual for IV-derived values and 0.005 points for 25-delta-derived points (R-62 is the upper bound); rows that cannot meet them are non-gating with the reason stated.
13. Third-batch rules (R-141 to R-150, from the PR 104 oracle review and the author-4 review): parameter names are `x` (lower-case) and `tail` in {top, bottom} for percentile forms (R-143); `X` is `x`; V5 `pair` values "9-30" and "30-91"; V10 `condition` in {change, sign_change} (R-135). Partial assertions (R-144): a row with null for `labels` or `coverage` is a harness-only partial assertion; null means NOT asserted. History load scans RECORD-MAJOR, as M2 (line by line, each record checked at its append against the list), so the pair (`duplicate_session`, `out_of_order_append`) is a fifth equivalent swap (R-148). Positions 14 and 15 of the request list apply whenever the request carries the parameter and the output uses it; rows for them sit on an output that uses thresholds, not on M3A-0 (R-149). The unit key follows the M1 runner `unit_key` format "%s/%02d/%03d" (underlying/session/cutoff) (R-147). Performance workload facts (R-150): 71 distinct spots across the 78 cutoffs (so a spot-keyed cache cannot pass M3A-B11 by accident); the two perf thresholds R03 (30 percent) and R07 (5 s and 512 MB) cite M2-B16 or are marked proposals.
14. OWNER DECISION 2 (2026-10-30, overrides the earlier 30-day-only recommendation) (R-151 to R-154): V3 and V4 implement BOTH forms. ATM form = the M1 `atm_iv` of the NEAREST ELIGIBLE expiry (M1 definition; eligible = expiry_days >= 1 and covered per R-20 and R-103; per-expiry ATM IVs stay in the frame); the design worker all-strike mean within 7 days is a recorded known difference. Request selector `iv_form` in {atm, 30d}, default atm, value code `iv_form_not_allowed` for any other string, `malformed_type` for a non-string, at position 13 of the request list after `delta_not_allowed`. The result echoes `iv_form` (R-154).
15. OWNER DECISION 3 (2026-10-30) (R-155 to R-161): the 25-delta points support BOTH conventions. Request `delta_convention` in {"spot", "forward"}, default "spot", read only by V8, V9, V10, V11, V21 and the 25-delta points of M3A-0 (V1 to V7 do not take it). Spot delta: call e^{-qT} N(d1), put e^{-qT} N(-d1); forward delta: call N(d1), put N(-d1) (no e^{-qT} factor); T = expiry_days/365, own IV, absolute value for puts, target 0.25 inclusive; bracketing and interpolation (R-09, R-10) unchanged on the chosen convention. The certified delta is computed for the requested convention only. Results carry `delta_convention` next to `iv_form`. Series: `skew25` (spot) and `skew25_fwd` (forward), skew-type derived scalars `<metric>_fwd`; `atm_iv`, `atm_iv_close`, `iv30`, `iv30_close`, `term_30_91` are convention-free. A prior or history record of the other convention is the structural code `convention_mismatch` (a record without the field is "spot"), at position 17 of the request list and at view read. V6, V7 take no convention; V9 and V10 history reads use the series of the requested convention.
